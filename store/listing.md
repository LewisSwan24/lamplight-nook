## 简体中文 (zh-CN)

### 名称

小灯屋

### 简短介绍

把 Claude 做的长任务，变成一家像素小咖啡馆里店员们的日常。

### 介绍

你在柜台上写下想做的事：一个小网页、一封信、一张表、一份计划、一本相册……店员们就开工了。
阿灯拿主意，帮手们出门查资料、定样子、做检查；黑板上写着 Claude 真正的计划，做完一项划掉一项。
遇到要你拿主意的地方，他们会来问你；删文件这类找不回来的事，一定先问过你，默认是“先不要”。

- 真实的进度：店员们的一举一动都来自 Claude 的真实事件，不是动画演给你看。
- 东西交给你：做好的东西放进作品架，随时打开，也可以再改一轮，旧版本留着。
- 可以交材料：把文件拖到柜台上，店员们只用副本，原件不动。
- 花多少心里有数：每次开工有花费上限，到了就停下来问你。
- 小店会长大：一起做完的委托越多，店里越热闹，还能自己布置墙纸、窗帘和地毯。
- 中文和英文两种界面。

### 使用前需要

- 电脑上装好 Claude Code（小灯屋会一步步带你装）。
- 一把你自己的 Anthropic API Key，在应用里粘贴即可，加密保存在这台电脑上。费用按你自己的 Key 计费，小灯屋不另外收费。
- 订阅了 Claude Max 或 Team 的话，方案每月附带的 Claude API 赠送额度也可以用在小灯屋：在 claude.ai 的账单设置里把额度关联到一个 Claude Console 组织，再用这个组织创建的 API Key 就行。

### 隐私

小灯屋不收集你的任何数据，没有统计、没有广告，也没有我们的服务器。你的设置、Key 和作品都只在你的电脑上。

## English (en)

### Name

Lamplight Nook

### Short description

Long AI tasks from Claude, played out as a day in a small pixel-art café.

### Description

Write what you want at the counter: a small web page, a letter, a table, a plan, a photo album... and the staff get to work.
Wick decides what to do, helpers go out to look things up, pick a look and check the work, and the blackboard shows Claude's
real plan, ticking items off as they are done. When something is yours to decide, they come and ask; anything that can't be
undone, such as deleting files, always waits for you, and the default is "not now".

- Real progress: every move the staff make comes from Claude's real events, not a canned animation.
- Work you keep: finished pieces go on the works shelf, ready to open, and can go through another round with the old version kept.
- Hand over files: drop them on the counter; the staff work on copies and never touch the originals.
- Spending you can see: each commission has a spending cap, and the shop stops and asks when it is reached.
- A shop that grows: the more commissions you finish together, the livelier it gets, and you can choose the walls, curtains and rug.
- In English and Simplified Chinese.

### What you need

- Claude Code installed on the computer (the shop walks you through it).
- Your own Anthropic API key, pasted in the app and stored encrypted on this computer. Usage is billed to your key; Lamplight Nook charges nothing extra.
- On Claude Max or Team? The monthly Claude API credits that come with your plan work in Lamplight Nook too: link them to a Claude Console organization in your claude.ai billing settings, then use an API key from that organization.

### Privacy

Lamplight Nook collects none of your data: no analytics, no ads, and no servers of ours. Your settings, key and work stay on your computer.
