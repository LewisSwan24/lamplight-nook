## 小灯屋 1.0.0

小灯屋到 1.0 了。进度随时查得到，数据更稳，店里也更像一家真的店。

**进度和花费**
- **一张卡看全进度。** 点柜台、黑板或时钟，打开的都是同一张卡：用了多久、这一轮的上限、现在在做什么、计划的每一步、谁在做什么。还可以插句话或者喊停。
- **喊停不白费。** 叫停的委托会告诉你做到了哪里，下次开店也能接着做。
- **上限按轮算。** 委托单、设置和停止卡都写明“这一轮”，停止卡显示的也是这一轮花了多少。
- **按月看花费。** 设置里能看到每个月花了多少、做了几单。

**作品和数据**
- **作品都留着。** 以前只记最近二十单，现在一件不丢。作品架可以搜索，也可以按种类看。
- **挪走的作品能找回来。** 文件夹挪了位置，把新位置指给店里就行。
- **数据坏了不会被盖掉。** 读不了的文件先放到一边，用上次开店时的副本顶上，阿灯会告诉你。
- **“全部忘掉”说到做到。** 运行记录会删掉，账本里也不再留委托的名字。
- **日期按你的本地时间算。**

**安全**
- 读委托文件夹以外的文件，一定先问你。
- 网页和文件里的内容只算材料。里面如果叫 Claude 运行什么、读别的文件或者改店里的规矩，它不会照做，还会在总结里说明。
- 点“打开”只会打开文档。程序、脚本和快捷方式只显示所在的文件夹。
- 安装版不能再拿来运行别的代码。

**小店**
- 店员、街坊、你和小猫都会绕着家具走，走到桌子后面只露出头。
- 前门留在临街的墙上，后门搬到下面那面墙，正对花园的小路。
- 做过什么，店里就添什么：相册、成摞的书、挂信的板子、日历、账本、旧电脑。老周、陈婆婆和小禾也会因为这些来串门。
- 花园的菜地跟着作品长，每做完三件冒一棵苗。窗外也会有人走过。
- 店里的东西点了都有回应，而且不花钱。

**好读好用**
- 工具栏换成六个像素图标，设置重新分了类，所有卡片的按钮和关闭方式都一样。
- 第一次来有人带着看。
- 文字和背景的对比度至少 4.5:1。读屏软件能听到每张卡是什么。
- Windows 缩放到 125%、150% 时，像素依然整齐。

**还有这些限制**
- 中途叫停的那一轮，Claude Code 不报费用，所以本月花费里没有这部分，设置里也写明了。准确数字看 Anthropic Console。

**安装**
- `Lamplight-Nook-Setup-1.0.0.exe`：网站安装包（未签名，SmartScreen 里点“更多信息”→“仍要运行”）。直接装在旧版上面即可，数据都会保留。
- `Lamplight-Nook-1.0.0-test.msix`：商店测试包，测试发布者，没有签名，Windows 可能拒绝安装。它能不能看到现有数据也没有验过，平时用请选上面的安装包。

## Lamplight Nook 1.0.0

Lamplight Nook reaches 1.0. You can check on the work at any time, your data is safer, and the shop feels more like a real one.

**Progress and spending**
- **One card for work under way.** The counter, the blackboard and the clock all open the same card: how long it has taken, the limit for this round, what is happening now, every step of the plan, and who is doing what. You can add a word or call it off from there.
- **Stopping doesn't waste the work.** A stopped commission shows what it managed, and you can carry on with it next time.
- **The limit is per round, and says so.** The commission form, settings and the stop card all say "this round", and the stop card shows what the round spent.
- **Month by month.** Settings shows what each month cost and on how many commissions.

**Your work and your data**
- **Nothing drops off the shelf.** The shop used to remember the last twenty commissions. It keeps them all now, and you can search the shelf or look at one kind of work.
- **A work that moved can be found again.** Show the shop the new folder and it picks the work back up.
- **A damaged file no longer costs you the file.** One that can't be read is put aside, the copy from when the shop last opened takes its place, and Wick tells you.
- **Forget everything means everything.** Records of runs are deleted, and the ledger no longer keeps the names of your commissions.
- **Dates are the ones on your wall.**

**Safety**
- Reading outside the commission folder always asks first.
- Pages and files are material to work from. If one tells Claude to run something, read other files or change the shop's rules, it doesn't, and says so in the summary.
- "Open" only opens documents. Programs, scripts and shortcuts are shown in their folder instead.
- The installed app can no longer be borrowed to run other code.

**The shop**
- The staff, the neighbours, you and the cat walk around the furniture, and someone behind the table shows only their head.
- The front door stays on the street wall, and the back door moves to the bottom wall, right above the garden path.
- What you make shows up in the shop: albums, stacks of books, a board of letters, a calendar, a ledger, an old computer. Old Zhou, Granny Chen and Xiaohe drop in because of it.
- The garden's beds grow with the work, a sprout for every three works finished. People pass the windows too.
- Everything in the shop answers a click, and it costs nothing.

**Easier to read and use**
- Six pixel icons on the toolbar, settings sorted into sections, and every card behaves the same way.
- A newcomer gets shown round.
- All text measures at least 4.5:1 against what is behind it. A screen reader is told what each card is.
- Pixels stay even when Windows scales to 125% or 150%.

**Known limits**
- Claude Code reports no cost for a round stopped partway, so the monthly figure leaves that part out, and settings says so. The Anthropic Console has the exact figure.

**Install**
- `Lamplight-Nook-Setup-1.0.0.exe`: the website installer (unsigned; in SmartScreen choose "More info", then "Run anyway"). Install it over the old version; your data stays.
- `Lamplight-Nook-1.0.0-test.msix`: the Store test package, with a test publisher and no signature, so Windows may refuse to install it. Whether it can see your existing data has not been checked, so use the installer above for everyday use.
